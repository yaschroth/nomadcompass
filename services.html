    How to read this

    Currently 48012 providers across 417 cities, 14 service types and 56 languages. Nothing goes in without a source, and the source is named on every card.

    Each card carries a tier saying how we know, because that matters more than the claim itself:

    • Checked with themWe wrote to the provider and it answered, confirming its own entry and the languages it works in. The card says on what date. This is the only tier where the claim comes from the business itself in answer to a question rather than from something it published or something a third party published about it, which is why these are listed first.
    • Official listA government body, embassy or consulate publishes this provider on a list defined by working language. Published by someone with nothing to sell, which is what makes it strong, and usually some months old, which is why a provider that answered us this year is listed above it.
    • Says so itselfThe provider's own website states the language. Reliable about intent, unverified about fluency.
    • Directory onlyOnly a third-party directory or aggregator states it. Weakest tier; these have a commercial interest in the listing. Many make a roster-level claim ("the clinics we list speak English") rather than one about the named business, and the row's note says which.

    What this data is and is not, including who publishes these lists and on what terms, is set out in the terms.
