Deciding between Sydney and Melbourne for your next remote-work base? On the overall Nomad Score, Sydney and Melbourne are neck and neck at 8.9. On budget, Melbourne is the more affordable of the two at roughly $2,740 a month versus $3,490 in Sydney. Below is the full side-by-side across all 13 factors we rate, plus where each city pulls ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sydney or Melbourne cheaper for digital nomads?

Melbourne is the cheaper base, around $2,740 a month versus $3,490 in Sydney, a difference of about $750.

Which has better WiFi, Sydney or Melbourne?

Both score 8/10 for WiFi, so connectivity is comparable.

Which is safer, Sydney or Melbourne?

Both rate 8/10 on safety.

Sydney or Melbourne: which is better overall for remote work?

They tie on the overall Nomad Score (8.9/10), so it comes down to your priorities.
