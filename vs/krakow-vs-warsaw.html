Deciding between Krakow and Warsaw for your next remote-work base? On the overall Nomad Score, Krakow edges ahead, 7.8 to 7.1. On budget, Krakow is the more affordable of the two at roughly $1,800 a month versus $2,200 in Warsaw. Below is the full side-by-side across all 13 factors we rate, plus where each city pulls ahead.
