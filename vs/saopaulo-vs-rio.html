Deciding between São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro for your next remote-work base? On the overall Nomad Score, Rio de Janeiro edges ahead, 6.9 to 6.7. On budget, Rio de Janeiro is the more affordable of the two at roughly $1,600 a month versus $2,200 in São Paulo. Below is the full side-by-side across all 13 factors we rate, plus where each city pulls ahead.
