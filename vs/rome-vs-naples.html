Deciding between Rome and Naples for your next remote-work base? On the overall Nomad Score, Rome edges ahead, 6.9 to 6.1. Frequently asked questions

Is Rome or Naples cheaper for digital nomads?

Naples is the cheaper base, around $2,300 a month versus $3,000 in Rome, a difference of about $700.

Rome scores higher for WiFi (6/10 versus 5/10).

Which is safer, Rome or Naples?

Rome rates higher on safety (6/10 versus 4/10).

Rome takes the overall Nomad Score (6.9/10 versus 6.1/10), though the right pick depends on what you value most.
