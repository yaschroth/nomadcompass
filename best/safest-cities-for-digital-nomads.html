The Safest Cities for Digital Nomads

The bases where a late-night walk home feels like a non-event, ranked for personal safety and low crime.

Safety shapes almost everything else about nomad life: how late you stay out, whether you carry a laptop openly in a cafe, how relaxed you feel arriving somewhere new alone. A base where petty theft is rare and violent crime rarer still frees up mental bandwidth you would otherwise spend watching your bag or planning routes around certain streets. That freedom matters most for solo travellers and women, who often carry the sharpest awareness of a place.

Every city below earns a perfect 10 on our safety measure, so read the order as a tiebreak on overall livability rather than a gap in security. Weigh the safety comfort against the trade-offs each place asks of you, cost, climate, or how much English gets you through the day, and pick the one whose compromises you can live with.

Cities are ranked by their personal-safety score across our 410-city index, with ties broken by the overall Nomad Score. Explore the data yourself on the comparison tool or browse all 410 city guides.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the safest city in the world for digital nomads?

Tokyo tops our ranking. It matches a perfect safety score with the strongest overall livability of any city on the list, thanks to a rare combination of low petty crime, near-absent violent crime, and a social culture where walking alone at night, even for solo women, feels genuinely routine rather than risky.

Are these cities safe for solo female travellers?

Yes. Every city here earns a perfect safety score, and each is regularly cited by solo women as comfortable for walking alone after dark. The Japanese, Nordic, and Swiss cities in particular are known for low harassment and high public trust. Standard awareness still applies, but the day-to-day baseline is unusually reassuring.

Does high safety mean these cities are expensive?

Not always. Zurich, Luxembourg, and Reykjavik run 5,000 dollars a month or more, but several Japanese and Finnish cities, including Sapporo, Oulu, Hiroshima, and Turku, deliver identical top-tier safety for around 2,600 to 2,800 dollars. Safety and cost are separate variables here, so you can prioritize security without a Swiss budget.
