Best Cities for Fast, Reliable WiFi

Where the connection holds through your morning standup, your afternoon uploads, and your late-night calls across time zones.

Nothing derails a remote career faster than a dropped video call or an upload that stalls at 80 percent. When your income depends on a stable connection, the quality of a city's internet stops being a background detail and becomes the foundation everything else sits on. The cities below earn their place because you can trust the connection: in your apartment, at a cafe, in a coworking space, and on mobile data when you need a backup.

We scored every city on connection reliability rather than just headline speed, weighing how well it handles calls, uploads, and always-on work. Read the list top to bottom for the safest bets, and use the cost and Nomad Score figures beside each entry to balance connectivity against climate, safety, and budget. A perfect network means little if the rest of the base does not fit your life.

Frequently asked questions

Which city has the most reliable internet for remote work?

Singapore and Seoul lead our ranking with perfect connectivity scores. Both offer fast, stable broadband, dependable cafe and coworking wifi, and strong mobile coverage that holds up even underground. For always-on work with calls across multiple time zones, either city removes connection reliability from your list of worries.

What internet speed do I need for video calls and uploads?

For smooth video calls you want a stable connection above roughly 10 Mbps upload, but consistency matters more than peak speed. A network that never drops at 30 Mbps beats a faster one that stalls during meetings. Every city on this list is chosen for reliability, not just headline numbers, which is what remote work actually depends on.

Should I rely on cafe wifi or get a mobile data plan?

Use both. Cafe and coworking wifi handles your main workday, while a local SIM with generous data gives you a backup for when a connection fails mid-call. In the top-ranked cities here, mobile coverage is strong enough to run an entire call on data if needed, which is worth the small monthly cost for peace of mind.
