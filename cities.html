Each city is rated on 13 categories using official data, global indices, and expert assessments. The Nomad Score is a calibrated composite of all 13 category scores (1-10 scale).

1
Climate
Temperature, humidity, rain days, and seasonal extremes. Optimized for remote work comfort (15-28°C ideal).
2
Cost
Monthly living expenses including rent, food, transport, and entertainment. Based on cost-of-living indices.
3
WiFi
Average internet speed and reliability. Based on global speed indices and coworking infrastructure.
4
Nightlife
Bars, clubs, live music, and social scene vibrancy. Based on venue density and reputation.
5
Nature
Access to parks, beaches, mountains, and outdoor activities. Based on proximity and diversity of nature.
6
Safety
Personal safety and crime rates. Based on crime indices and government travel advisories.
7
Food
Culinary scene quality and diversity. Based on cuisine reputation, recognition, and street food culture.
8
Community
Digital nomad presence and networking. Based on coworking spaces, meetups, and expat communities.
9
English
English proficiency among locals. Based on proficiency indices and tourism infrastructure.
10
Visa
Ease of obtaining a visa or staying long-term. Considers nomad visas, visa-free stays, and extensions.
11
Culture
Museums, galleries, historical sites, and cultural events. Based on heritage sites and cultural vibrancy.
12
Cleanliness
Street cleanliness, waste management, and overall city hygiene standards.
13
Air Quality
Air pollution levels and AQI. Based on global air-quality reports and health standards.
Score Scale
8-10: Excellent
6-7: Good
4-5: Average
1-3: Below Average
