The Nomad HQ City Index

The Best Cities for Digital Nomads

Every ranking below is drawn from the same index of 999 destinations, scored on the 13 factors that shape life as a remote worker. Pick the one that matches what you are optimizing for.

There is no single best city for remote work, only the best city for your priorities. These rankings break the question down one factor at a time, from the cheapest bases to the safest streets and the fastest internet, so you can start from what matters most to you and drill into the full guide from there.

Weather and climate

Best Cities for Year-Round Good Weather

Our ranking of the best digital nomad cities for great year-round weather: mild seasons, reliable sunshine, and warmth that keeps you outside and working well.

#1 Honolulu, United States

Best Cities for Winter Sun

Las Palmas, Cape Town and Tenerife top our list of bases that stay warm and dry from December to February, from 21C Canary days to a 27C Cape Town January.

#1 Las Palmas, Spain

Best Cities to Escape the Summer Heat

Lisbon, Porto and San Diego lead our ranking of bases that stay mild from June to August, with summer highs mostly between 20C and 28C and almost no rain.

#1 Lisbon, Portugal

Eternal Spring Cities for Digital Nomads

Medellín, Rarotonga and Oaxaca lead 15 nomad bases where every month averages 14C to 24C. Medellín's monthly averages never leave 22C to 24C.

#1 Medellín, Colombia

The Safest Cities for Digital Nomads
