Why we built this

Choosing where to base yourself as a remote worker usually means stitching together a dozen half-answers: a Reddit thread on visas, an outdated cost-of-living post, a listicle written by someone who never went. The advice is scattered, often sponsored, and rarely comparable city to city.

The Nomad HQ replaces that guesswork with one consistent system. We rate 410 cities on the same 13 categories, give each a calibrated Nomad Score, and show the full breakdown, so you can weigh Lisbon against Chiang Mai against Medellin on equal terms and decide based on what matters to you.

The Nomad Score

The headline score is not a plain average. It is a calibrated composite of the 13 categories, rescaled so the strongest and weakest cities genuinely stand out instead of clustering around the middle. You can browse and compare every city or match the ratings to your own priorities on the Nomad Wheel.

Independent by design

Trust only works if the ratings are honest, so we hold to a few firm rules:

  • No pay-for-ranking. Scores come from data and assessment, never from advertising or affiliate partnerships. No city can buy a better position.
  • Affiliate links are disclosed. Some links earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. That revenue keeps the site free and never influences a rating. See our affiliate disclosure.
  • Corrections are welcome. If a number looks wrong, tell us. Reader corrections keep the ratings honest and up to date.
