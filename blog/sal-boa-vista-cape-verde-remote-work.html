Boa Vista and Sal for Digital Nomads: Cape Verde Remote Work

Every international internet cable that reaches Cape Verde comes ashore in Praia. Sal and Boa Vista, where most holiday flights land, are fed from the capital by a domestic submarine ring that Cabo Verde Telecom first laid in 1997, and Ookla's July 2026 figures put the national fixed broadband median at 38.6 Mbps. Weigh that before choosing Santa Maria or Sal Rei as a digital nomad base: the beaches are on the eastern islands, the infrastructure is on Santiago.

The visa does not change with the island. The Cape Verde Remote Working Program asks for an average bank balance of about $1,710 over six months and grants six months, renewable once. This guide covers what does change: connectivity, power, rent, flights, wind and medical backup. For the capital itself, read our Praia digital nomad guide. Dollar figures use the ECB exchange rate of 25 September 2026, $1.1403 per euro, and the escudo, pegged at 110.265 to the euro.

Sal and Boa Vista vs Praia: what a digital nomad trades

Sal is flat, dry and built around its airport. Turismo de Cabo Verde, the national tourism body, calls Amílcar Cabral International the busiest airport in the country, six minutes by car from Espargos, the island capital and commercial centre, which had 17,081 residents at the 2010 census. Santa Maria, at the southern tip, grew around the country's first resort hotel, the Morabeza, opened in 1967, and counted 6,258 people in the same census.

Boa Vista is emptier still: the least densely populated island in the archipelago, with most of its people in Sal Rei, which had 5,778 residents in 2010. The airport sits about five kilometres southeast of town.

Praia is a working capital by comparison, with the ministries, the main public hospital, the four coworking spaces on our city page and every international cable. In our own neighbourhood ratings, Santa Maria takes the highest price tier of the five Cape Verde areas we cover ($$$), Sal Rei sits with Praia at $$, and we describe Boa Vista's infrastructure as very limited. Those are editorial judgements; the sections below set out the sourced facts.

The Cape Verde Remote Working Program, as of September 2026

The Ministry of Tourism and Transport launched the program on 4 December 2020. Its official page, published by Turismo de Cabo Verde, sets the terms as of September 2026: six months, renewable for six more, open to citizens of Europe, North America, the Portuguese-speaking CPLP countries and the West African ECOWAS bloc.

The financial test is a balance, not a salary. An individual needs a minimum average bank balance of about $1,710 over the last six months; a family needs about $3,080. The requirement is set in euros, so its dollar value moves with the exchange rate, which is why figures quoted around the web disagree. Check the current euro amount on the program page before you apply.

You also need a passport valid for six months, an accommodation booking, and travel and health insurance covering medical evacuation and repatriation of remains. The visa fee is about $23 per person, due again on renewal. Turismo de Cabo Verde states that remote workers on the program are exempt from Cape Verdean income tax.

  1. Apply through the online form linked from the program page, with passport, six months of bank statements, insurance and booking.
  2. Wait about two weeks. The tourism body advises against travelling before approval.
  3. Receive an approval letter from the Instituto do Turismo de Cabo Verde and a visa approval declaration from the Direção de Estrangeiros e Fronteiras, the border directorate.
  4. Pay the visa and airport fees and carry both letters when you land on Sal, Boa Vista or Santiago.

Our digital nomad visa comparison sets this six-month term against other countries' income floors and durations.

Arriving on Sal or Boa Vista: EASE and the airport security tax

Visitors from visa-exempt countries, including the UK and the EU, can stay 30 days without a visa but must pre-register on the EASE platform at least five days before arrival and pay the airport security tax (TSA) of about $35. Children aged one and under are exempt. The UK Foreign Office notes a change from 1 July 2026: arrive without the pre-registration, or without proof of it, and you pay double at border control.

Two official figures do not match. The Remote Working Program page lists an airport fee of about $39, paid once on arrival, while the tourism body's entry page and Portugal's foreign ministry give the security tax as about $35. Budget the higher one and keep the receipt with your approval letters.

The tourist tax on Sal and Boa Vista accommodation

Cape Verde charges a tourist contribution on nights in hotels and similar establishments. It was introduced in May 2013 at about $2.28 a night for guests aged 16 and over, for up to 10 nights, and the 2023 State Budget raised it by 25 percent to about $2.85. It is paid at the accommodation, in cash or by card, and funds the government's MAIS social programme.

For a remote worker the cap matters more than the rate: ten nights is about $29 per stay, so a month in one aparthotel costs the same in tax as a ten-night holiday. Enforcement targets exactly these islands. In April 2025 the economic inspectorate, IGAE, announced tighter checks on accommodation on both islands, and a listing platform may add the charge at checkout.

Internet on Sal and Boa Vista for remote work

TeleGeography's Submarine Cable Map explains the islands' position. Three international systems land in Cape Verde, all at Praia: WACS (2012), EllaLink to Portugal and Brazil (2021) and SHARE to Dakar (2023). Sal connects at Murdeira bay and Boa Vista at Sal Rei, both on Cabo Verde Telecom's domestic ring, so the eastern islands reach those cables only through the ring, while Praia has three systems to fall back on.

Measured speeds exist only at national level. The Speedtest Global Index gave Cabo Verde a median fixed download of 38.57 Mbps and upload of 14.30 Mbps for July 2026, ranking it 127th, and shows no mobile median at all. We rate wifi 5 out of 10 in Praia and Mindelo. No island-level figure for Santa Maria or Sal Rei exists that we would stand behind.

Two operators cover both islands. Cabo Verde Telecom announced in November 2022 that its mobile arm, CV Móvel, would merge into the parent, and now sells fibre and mobile as Alou; Unitel T+ is the Angolan-owned rival. A 2023 quality study by the regulator ARME with Portugal's ANACOM, reported by the broadcaster Tiver, put Unitel T+ ahead on Sal, Boa Vista and Santiago.

Numbeo contributors put an unlimited home line of 60 Mbps or more at about $54 a month nationally, a rough figure. Ask a landlord for a speed test on the flat's own line before paying a deposit, and buy SIMs from both operators for a dual-SIM hotspot. None of the coworking spaces on our Cape Verde city pages is on either island, so plan to work from the rental.

Power and water supply on Boa Vista and Sal

Sal is on the national utility, Electra, with a private producer, Águas de Ponta Preta, also generating power there. Boa Vista is the one island outside Electra: its electricity and water come from Águas e Energia de Boavista (AEB), a public-private company. Sockets are European Type C and F at 230 volts and 50 Hz.

Cabeólica runs four wind parks in the archipelago, and in December 2025 it inaugurated 26 MWh of battery storage at an expanded wind farm, which ESS News reports is already improving frequency regulation. That is a grid-level gain, not a promise for one apartment, so a small UPS for the router is cheap insurance. Portugal's foreign ministry advises drinking only bottled water, without ice, so ask whether rent includes water and electricity.

Rent and living costs in Santa Maria, Espargos and Sal Rei

No rent survey for Sal or Boa Vista deserves a number. Numbeo's Santa Maria page rests on one contributor (42 entries in 12 months), its Sal page on two, and its Boa Vista page had no entries when we checked in September 2026. We will not turn that into a budget, and you should distrust anyone who does.

National data is firmer, if still rough. Across 13 Numbeo contributors, a central one-bedroom in Cape Verde averages about $427 a month and one outside a centre $254, with utilities for an 85 square metre flat about $149. Our own estimates are $1,400 a month for Praia and $950 for Mindelo, editorial estimates rather than survey results.

The shape of island prices is clearer than their level. Santa Maria is built around resort hotels, and Turismo de Cabo Verde notes that euros are widely accepted, especially on these two islands. Espargos, with nearly three times Santa Maria's population, is where to look for a monthly rental priced for residents. On Boa Vista, Sal Rei is the only town of any size. Ask for the monthly price in writing and in escudos, and compare it with the same flat's weekly holiday rate.

Weighing the islands against the mainland? Put Praia next to Dakar, which Air Senegal links to Sal, in our side-by-side city comparison: budget, wifi and all 13 category scores in one view.

International flights into Sal and Boa Vista airports

Sal is the gateway. Flights connect it with Lisbon on TAP and Cabo Verde Airlines, with Frankfurt, Munich and Düsseldorf on TUIfly, and with Dakar on Air Senegal, alongside seasonal routes from the UK, the Benelux countries, Scandinavia and Italy. Turismo de Cabo Verde puts Lisbon about four hours away and the UK six to seven. Cabo Verde Airlines also lists Providence in the US and Recife in Brazil, with online check-in from Praia, São Vicente and Sal.

Boa Vista's Aristides Pereira airport is the country's third busiest, and its timetable is almost all European leisure traffic: TUI from the UK, Germany, Belgium and the Netherlands, Neos from Italy and TAP from Lisbon, several of them seasonal. It has no scheduled link to mainland Africa, so a Dakar or Casablanca trip routes through Sal or Praia.

Getting between Sal, Boa Vista and Praia

Domestic flights changed hands in 2026. Bestfly, the operator named in many older guides, suspended operations in April 2024 and never resumed; Cabo Verde Airlines flew the network after that, and on 29 March 2026 the new state carrier CVsky took over inter-island flights with ATR 72-600 turboprops.

CVsky's site advertises Sal to Boa Vista from about $91 and Santiago to Sal from about $123, as of September 2026, and Turismo de Cabo Verde puts inter-island flights at 20 to 45 minutes. CVsky warned in April 2026 that adverse weather can disrupt flights.

The sea is slower. CV Interilhas runs the Redonda Line twice a week through São Vicente, São Nicolau, Sal, Boa Vista and Santiago: about 3 hours from Sal to Boa Vista and 8 from Boa Vista to Santiago, on the 250-passenger D. Tututa. Sailings are adjusted daily for weather, and the UK Foreign Office warns that sea conditions can be dangerous. Fly anything with a deadline attached.

Wind, rain and the best season to work from Sal and Boa Vista

Both islands are drier than Praia. Open-Meteo's reanalysis for 2019 to 2023 gives Santa Maria about 100 mm of rain a year and Sal Rei about 140 mm, against about 215 mm for Praia, almost all of it from August to October. The UK Foreign Office dates the rainy season from mid-August to mid-October and warns of floods and landslides on some islands.

In the same modelled data, the daily mean wind at Santa Maria peaks in February at about 12 knots (23 km/h) and drops to about 7.5 knots (14 km/h) in August, and Sal Rei follows the same curve. Daytime highs run from about 25°C in February to 30°C in October.

For desk work, March to June is the steadiest stretch: dry, still breezy and past the winter holiday peak. December to March brings the strongest wind, which is why the kitesurfers come, plus the Saharan dust haze our Praia guide describes. August to October is the hottest stretch and the one when rain can cut roads and flights.

Healthcare and safety for digital nomads on Sal and Boa Vista

The UK Foreign Office describes healthcare in Cape Verde as very basic and limited, says the GHIC card is not accepted and gives 130 as the ambulance number. Portugal's foreign ministry lists the Hospital Dr. Agostinho Neto in Praia among the main hospitals, plus a hospital line on each island: +238 241 11 62 on Sal and +238 251 11 67 on Boa Vista.

The visa already assumes the gap, since it requires insurance covering medical evacuation. The Foreign Office's own example is a quad-bike accident, after which evacuation is very likely. It also notes that UK health authorities investigated a rise in Shigella sonnei and Salmonella among travellers returning from Cape Verde.

Crime is generally low, in the Foreign Office's words, but pickpocketing, bag snatching and muggings occur particularly in Praia, Sal and Boa Vista. We rate Praia 7 out of 10 for safety. On both islands the authorised taxis are blue and yellow, and the advice is to swim only where a lifeguard is on duty, because currents and undertow are strong.

Which Cape Verde island suits your remote work

Choose Sal for the most flights, the shops and services of Espargos, and Santa Maria's beach and wind a short ride away. It is the only one of the two with scheduled links to mainland Africa, and CVsky connects it to Praia, São Vicente and Boa Vista.

Choose Boa Vista if quiet is the point and your work tolerates one connection path and a thin flight map. Choose Praia if your income depends on your connection: it has every international cable, the coworking spaces and the main hospital, and Sal is one short flight away. The full numbers, with all 13 category scores, are on our Praia city profile; if culture matters more than beaches, look at Mindelo on São Vicente, which we rate 8 for culture and 7 for nightlife.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can digital nomads live in Boa Vista, Cape Verde?

Yes, on the same Remote Working Program as the rest of the country: six months, renewable once, with an average bank balance of about $1,710 over six months. The limits are practical. Sal Rei had 5,778 residents at the 2010 census, its flights are mostly European leisure routes, and we have not verified a coworking space there.

Is there coworking in Sal Rei or Santa Maria for digital nomads?

We have not verified a dedicated coworking space on either island, and none of the spaces on our Cape Verde city pages is there. Plan to work from your rental, ask the landlord for a speed test on the flat's own line before paying, and carry SIMs from both CV Telecom and Unitel T+.

How fast is the internet on Sal and Boa Vista?

No reliable island-level figure exists. Ookla's national median for fixed broadband was 38.57 Mbps down and 14.30 Mbps up in July 2026. Both islands reach the international cables through Cabo Verde Telecom's domestic submarine ring from Praia, so test a line before you commit to a lease.

How much money do I need for the Cape Verde remote working visa?

A minimum average bank balance of about $1,710 over the last six months for an individual, or about $3,080 for a family, as of September 2026. The visa fee is about $23 per person, due again on renewal, plus an airport fee listed at about $39. Processing takes about two weeks.

How do I get from Sal to Boa Vista?

By air, CVsky flies Sal to Boa Vista with fares advertised from about $91 as of September 2026, and inter-island flights take 20 to 45 minutes. By sea, the CV Interilhas Redonda Line takes about three hours and sails twice a week, adjusted daily for weather.

Do I pay the Cape Verde tourist tax on a monthly stay in Sal or Boa Vista?

In hotels and similar establishments, yes: about $2.85 a night for guests aged 16 and over, capped at 10 nights, so about $29 per stay. It is paid at the accommodation. Inspections of accommodation on both islands were tightened in 2025, so expect it to be charged.

Is Sal or Boa Vista better than Praia for remote work?

For beach, wind and direct European flights, yes. For connectivity, healthcare and coworking, no: every international cable, the main hospital and all the coworking spaces we list are in Praia. Of the two islands, Sal is stronger, with the busiest airport and a commercial town in Espargos.

When is the best time for digital nomads to work from Sal and Boa Vista?

March to June: dry, breezy and past the winter holiday peak. December to March brings the strongest wind for kitesurfing along with Saharan dust haze. The rainy season runs from mid-August to mid-October, when rain can disrupt roads, flights and ferries.
