Hiring a Lawyer in Antalya: When You Need One, What It Costs

Most foreigners in Antalya never need a lawyer for a residence permit. You can sign the application form yourself, the e-İkamet appointment is free, and the migration authority's own checklist tells you to ignore anyone who charges for one. A lawyer in Antalya earns the fee when the state says no, or when serious money moves: a refused permit gives you 60 days to sue, a deportation order gives you seven, and a property purchase sends your savings through a land registry office that works in Turkish.

This guide separates the jobs a Turkish lawyer (avukat) actually does for foreigners from the ones you can do alone, and prices them against the bar's official floor: $82 for the first hour of an office consultation under the 2025 to 2026 minimum fee tariff. Every lira figure is converted at the Central Bank of Türkiye's buying rate of $1 = 48.79 TL on 25 September 2026.

When a foreigner needs a lawyer in Antalya, and when not

Turkish law draws the line in Article 35 of the Attorneys Act, Law 1136. Giving opinions on legal questions, representing people before courts and drafting case papers for others are reserved to lawyers registered with a bar. The same article lets anyone who is able to sue prepare their own papers, file their own case and pursue their own business with a public office. That splits a foreigner's to-do list cleanly.

You can handle these alone: a first residence permit, its renewal, address registration, and a lease signed at a notary. A property purchase does not legally require a lawyer either. The land registry deals with the buyer and seller, or with whoever holds their power of attorney, and brings in a sworn translator if one side does not speak Turkish.

A lawyer becomes the sensible choice, and for court work the only practical one, in four situations: a residence refusal or cancellation you want to challenge; a deportation decision; a property purchase where you cannot read the title record yourself or will not be in the country; and a company whose articles of association have to be right the first time. Divorce, criminal charges and inheriting Turkish property belong on the same list. The rest is paperwork.

Residence permits in Antalya: what a lawyer adds to e-İkamet

Staying beyond your visa or visa-free period means holding one of the residence permits in Law 6458, the Law on Foreigners and International Protection. For most remote workers that is the short-term permit (kısa dönem ikamet izni), granted for up to two years at a time under Article 31 on grounds that include tourism, owning a home and setting up business connections. You apply online at e-İkamet, then take the paper file to the Antalya Provincial Directorate of Migration Management on your appointment day.

The document list is short and exacting, per Göç İdaresi's own checklist (as of September 2026): the signed form, a passport copy, two biometric photos, fee receipts, health insurance covering the whole permit period, proof of means and proof of address. For a rental, the address proof is a notarised copy of the lease that names the landlord. On the tourism ground, a single applicant must show income of at least one and a half times the net minimum wage, evidenced over at least the last three months.

The net minimum wage for 2026 is about $575 a month, so that floor is about $863. A family applying together needs one minimum wage per person instead. If you apply as a homeowner, the home must have been worth at least $200,000 when you bought it, must be a dwelling, and must not be rented out.

The fees are modest. The permit card costs $20 in 2026 for every nationality. The permit fee itself depends on your passport, because the Treasury sets it by reciprocity: the default rate is $46 for each month after the first, and citizens of ten countries, including Ireland, Denmark and Czechia, pay none. Once a first permit is approved, you have 20 working days to register your address.

Two details catch people in Antalya specifically. The provincial directorate still publishes a list of neighbourhoods closed to new residence registrations: Liman, Hurma and Sarısu in Konyaaltı, Topçular in Muratpaşa, two industrial-zone neighbourhoods in Döşemealtı, and Kargıcak, Mahmutlar, Kestel and Avsallar in Alanya. Local press reported the four Alanya neighbourhoods reopened in June 2026, but the directorate's file has not changed, so check a specific address before you sign a lease there. And a residence permit does not give you the right to work, which the same checklist states outright.

When an immigration lawyer in Antalya earns the fee

A refusal, a non-renewal or a cancellation is notified to you, your legal representative or your lawyer (Law 6458, Article 25). You can challenge it at the administrative court within 60 days of written notice, the general deadline in Article 7 of the Administrative Procedure Law. Since a November 2024 amendment, these short-term permit cases are treated as complete once the authority has filed its defence, and a hearing is at the court's discretion. The written petition is, in practice, the whole case.

Deportation runs on a far shorter clock. Under Article 53, as amended in 2024, you, your legal representative or your lawyer have seven days from notification to apply to the administrative court. You are not removed while that case is pending unless you consent, the court must decide within 15 days of the file being complete, and its ruling is final. Seven days is too short to find, vet and brief a lawyer from scratch.

The other legitimate use is filing on your behalf. The e-İkamet checklist says the form may be signed by the foreigner, their legal representative, or a lawyer holding a power of attorney. If a renewal falls due while you are travelling, that is how it gets filed without you.

Turkey's digital nomad visa and the Antalya residence permit

Citizens of 36 listed countries, including every EU state except Cyprus, the UK, the US and Canada, can apply for a Digital Nomad Identification Certificate through the GoTürkiye programme. The published requirements (as of September 2026) are an age of 21 to 55, a university degree, and remote income of at least $3,000 a month or $36,000 a year from an employer or clients outside Türkiye. Australia is not on the list.

It is an application you file yourself, and a lawyer adds little unless it is refused. Our nomad visa finder carries the same threshold next to the other countries that run a remote-work visa.

Buying property in Antalya: where a Turkish lawyer fits at the tapu office

Citizens of the 185 countries on the 2012 list issued under the Land Registry Law can buy almost any kind of real estate, within fixed limits: no more than 30 hectares per person nationwide, no more than 10 percent of a district's private land in foreign hands, and nothing inside a military prohibited or security zone. Land bought without a building carries a duty to submit a development project to the relevant ministry within two years, or it is liquidated.

Two steps that older guides still describe no longer exist. The valuation report is not required for an ordinary foreign purchase: the land registry now asks for one only in citizenship-by-investment cases, where a report by the state valuation company GEDAŞ produces an Amount Determination Certificate (TTB) valid for six months. And military clearance is no longer a separate wait. Since circulars of 2017, the zones are recorded on the land register in all 81 provinces, and the registry office decides directly from the record.

What replaced them is the foreign currency purchase certificate (döviz alım belgesi, DAB). The purchase price must be sold as foreign currency to a Turkish bank, which sends the certificate to the land registry by registered e-mail before the sale, and the lira value on the certificate becomes the declared price on the deed. According to the land registry's foreign-buyer FAQ, there is no exemption for long-term residents or for spouses of Turkish citizens.

The main registry cost is the title deed fee (tapu harcı): 2 percent of the declared price from the buyer and 2 percent from the seller, on a price that cannot be below the municipal property-tax value (Fees Law 492, tariff 4, item 20a). On a $200,000 flat that is $4,000 each.

Add a fixed revolving-fund fee, built on a 2026 base of $46 multiplied by a local coefficient, and compulsory earthquake insurance on the building. If the sales contract moves the seller's 2 percent onto you, you pay $8,000, so read that clause before paying a deposit.

None of the lawyer's work here is legally required, but it is the part where a mistake costs more than the fee. A property lawyer reads the title record for mortgages and seizures, confirms the seller is the registered owner, reviews the sales agreement and deposit terms, and, holding your power of attorney, handles the DAB and signs at the registry if you are abroad. A foreign buyer who does not speak Turkish needs a sworn translator at the signing whether or not a lawyer attends.

For scale: our Antalya city guide lists central purchase prices around $2,030 per square metre (Numbeo, July 2026), so the $200,000 homeowner-permit floor buys roughly 100 square metres in the centre. Citizenship by purchase needs a property worth $400,000, bought outright rather than as a share, with a three-year no-sale annotation on the title.

Power of attorney: letting an Antalya lawyer act while you are abroad

A power of attorney (vekaletname) is how a lawyer files your permit, signs at the land registry or registers a company without you in the room. Made in Türkiye, it is drawn up at a notary (noter), and under Article 74 of the Notaries Law a sworn translator must be present if you do not speak Turkish. Bring your passport and the lawyer's full details, and budget for the translator as well as the notary.

Made abroad, it has to pass the land registry's rules for foreign powers of attorney. From a country that is party to the Hague Apostille Convention, it needs an apostille from that country's competent authority, physically or digitally tied to the document. It must carry your photograph with the seal over it, must be in the issuing country's language (a bilingual Turkish version is accepted), and must be drawn up by the notary rather than being a bare certification of your signature.

From a non-Hague country, the chain is the issuing authority, a higher authority such as the foreign ministry, and then the Turkish consulate, all inside that country. A document certified by a foreign notary and then stamped at that country's consulate inside Türkiye is not accepted. Electronic apostilles are accepted when the registry can verify them by QR code or website. The land registry's Berlin representation office also handles registry transactions without you travelling at all.

Write the power for the task. Name the property, list the acts (buying, paying the fees, receiving the deed) and add an end date if the notary allows it. A general power over all your affairs hands over far more than one purchase needs.

Sworn translation and apostille for Antalya lawyers and offices

Foreign documents in the Turkish system follow one rule, repeated throughout the e-İkamet checklist: an apostille from the issuing country plus a Turkish translation certified by a notary. Documents from countries outside the Apostille Convention need consular legalisation instead. Marriage and birth certificates, custody papers and criminal record extracts all go through this, and Göç İdaresi advises ordering a home-country criminal record at least three months before an investor-route application.

At the land registry, the translator must be authorised by the regional judicial commission. Our list of sworn translators in Antalya holds 24 across six languages, most taken from the Ministry of Justice's regional list. A good Antalya lawyer tells you at the first meeting which documents need this treatment. Otherwise you pay for translations twice.

Company setup in Antalya: what a Turkish company lawyer handles

A limited company (limited şirket) needs at least $1,025 of capital and a joint-stock company (anonim şirket) $5,124, after Presidential Decision 7887 of November 2023 raised both floors (Turkish Commercial Code, Articles 580 and 332). The articles of association are the document worth paying a lawyer to draft. The minimum tariff for drafting articles, mergers and similar commercial contracts is $430, and for taking a company through formation and its licences $1,230.

One situation makes a lawyer compulsory. Article 35 of the Attorneys Act obliges joint-stock companies whose capital is at least five times the statutory minimum to keep a contract lawyer, with an administrative fine of two months' gross minimum wage for every month without one. At today's minimum that threshold is $25,620, and the tariff sets the monthly floor for those mandatory retainers at $922. A small limited company has no such duty.

A company also opens a residence route. The short-term permit ground for establishing business or commercial connections requires a visa issued for that purpose by a Turkish consulate, plus notarised copies of the Turkish company's activity certificate, tax plate, trade registry gazette and signature circular. It does not let you work for your own company. That takes a work permit.

Work permits in Antalya: why a residence permit is not a licence to work

Under the International Labour Force Law, Law 6735, a work permit application goes to the Ministry of Labour from inside Türkiye or through a Turkish consulate abroad, and an authorised intermediary may file it. A first permit is tied to a specific employer and job and lasts at most one year. Renewals can be filed from 60 days before expiry and never after it: a late renewal is refused. A valid work permit also counts as a residence permit (Law 6458, Article 27).

The 2026 fees are $258 for a permit of up to one year plus the $20 card. An independent work permit for self-employed professionals costs $2,578. For a standard hire the employer can file directly. A lawyer helps when the application is refused, or when the structure is unusual, such as a foreigner employed by their own Turkish company.

How to verify a lawyer in Antalya is registered with the bar

Only Turkish citizens can be admitted as lawyers (Attorneys Act, Article 3), and only people on a bar's roll may use the title avukat. Using it without registration draws an administrative fine under Article 63. Foreign law firms may operate in Türkiye only as advisers on foreign and international law (Article 44). A British or German lawyer living in Antalya can advise you on home-country law, but cannot take your case to a Turkish court.

The Antalya Bar Association (Antalya Barosu) runs a public lawyer search on its website. Search the full name before paying anything, and ask for the bar registration number. A relocation consultant or estate agent can be useful for logistics, but opinions on your legal position and drafting documents for others are reserved to registered lawyers by Article 35. Anyone who charges to get you a residence appointment is selling something the state gives away.

Get the fee agreement in writing. Article 163 requires a lawyer's contract to name the specific legal service and the amount or value. A percentage fee is allowed but capped at 25 percent of the value in dispute (Article 164). And a quote below the minimum tariff is not a bargain: the same article prohibits agreeing a fee below it.

What a lawyer in Antalya costs: the 2025 to 2026 minimum fee tariff

The Union of Turkish Bar Associations (TBB) sets a nationwide floor, the Minimum Attorney Fee Tariff (Avukatlık Asgari Ücret Tarifesi), published in the Official Gazette. The current tariff appeared on 4 November 2025 (issue 33067) and took effect on publication. The one before it was published in October 2024, so expect a new one around the end of 2026. The items foreigners most often buy:

  • Office consultation, first hour: $82; each further hour $37
  • Consultation at your home or office, first hour: $143
  • Written legal opinion, first hour: $143; each further hour $72
  • Any petition, formal notice or protest: $123
  • Lease contract: $164
  • Company articles, mergers and other commercial contracts: $430
  • Will, inheritance contract and similar documents: $656
  • Registering, transferring or protecting a right, such as a title transfer: $246
  • Company formation, business licences, or a citizenship application: $1,230
  • Administrative court case: $615 without a hearing, $820 with one
  • Monthly retainer for a private client: $676

These are floors, not prices. A lawyer quoting for a residence refusal starts from the administrative court item, and one quoting a property closing from the $246 registration item plus hourly advice. Ask whether the quote includes KDV (Turkish VAT), and expect notary, translator and court charges on top, because the tariff prices the lawyer's work, not the state's fees.

Immigration, company or property: choosing the right Antalya lawyer

Antalya's lawyers who work with foreigners are mostly generalists. Of the 38 in our directory, 34 list property or real-estate work, 30 list company or commercial law, and 23 list immigration, residence or nationality law. Twenty-one list all three. The labels tell you little on their own. What separates one Antalya lawyer from another is volume in the specific procedure you need.

Ask questions with checkable answers. For a residence refusal: how many cases they filed at the Antalya administrative courts in the past year, and whether they can write the petition inside the 60-day window, or the seven-day one for deportation. For property: how many foreign-buyer closings they handled at an Antalya land registry office, and whether they run the DAB step themselves. For a company: whether they have drafted articles for a foreign-owned limited company before.

English is recorded for 34 of the 38 and German for 21. Four are based in Alanya, a separate district of the province, which matters if your flat or your file sits there; our Alanya city guide covers the town itself. For a wider field, our nationwide list of lawyers in Turkey holds 191 across eight cities, 82 of them in Istanbul.

Hiring a lawyer in Antalya: the short version

Do the residence permit yourself, and keep a vetted lawyer's number for the day it is refused, because the seven-day deportation deadline leaves no time to shop around. Hire one for a property purchase if you cannot read a Turkish title record or will not attend the signing, and write the power of attorney for that one purchase. Hire one to draft company articles, and treat it as compulsory only for a joint-stock company above the capital threshold.

Whoever you hire, check the name on the Antalya Bar's register, get the fee in writing, and remember that the tariff is the floor. For another base on this coast, the Bodrum digital nomad guide covers a smaller resort town to the west, where internet speed is the deciding factor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a lawyer for a residence permit in Antalya?

No. You apply on e-İkamet and sign the form yourself; a lawyer with a power of attorney may sign instead. Appointments are free, and Göç İdaresi warns against paying third parties for them. A lawyer matters when a permit is refused or cancelled, since you have 60 days to challenge it in the administrative court.

How much does a lawyer in Antalya charge for a consultation?

The legal floor is $82 for the first hour in the lawyer's office and $37 for each further hour, under the 2025 to 2026 TBB minimum fee tariff. A consultation at your home costs at least $143. Lawyers may charge more but may not agree to less.

How do I check that an Antalya lawyer is licensed?

Search the lawyer's full name on the Antalya Bar Association's public lawyer search and ask for their bar registration number. Only Turkish citizens on a bar's roll may practise and use the title avukat. Foreign law firms may advise only on foreign and international law, not represent you in Turkish courts.

Is a valuation report still required to buy property in Antalya?

Not for an ordinary purchase. The land registry dropped it for foreign buyers and now requires a GEDAŞ valuation, with a certificate valid six months, only when the purchase is for citizenship. Every foreign buyer still needs a foreign currency purchase certificate from a Turkish bank, and pays 2 percent title deed fee.

How long do I have to appeal a residence permit decision in Antalya?

A refusal, non-renewal or cancellation can be challenged at the administrative court within 60 days of written notice. A deportation decision is different: you or your lawyer have only seven days from notification to apply, and you cannot be removed while that case is pending unless you consent.

Can a lawyer in Antalya buy property for me with a power of attorney?

Yes. A power of attorney made at a Turkish notary, or abroad with an apostille and your sealed photograph, lets a lawyer sign at the land registry, pay the fees and receive the deed. Limit it to the named property and the acts needed, rather than granting a general power over your affairs.

Do lawyers in Antalya speak English?

Many who work with foreigners do. Of the 38 lawyers in our Antalya directory, 34 have English recorded on an official list and 21 German. Most entries come from the UK Foreign Office's list and the German consulate's list, and each card names its source so you can check it.
