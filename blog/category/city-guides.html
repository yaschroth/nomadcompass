City Guides

In-depth, field-tested guides to living and working remotely in specific cities, cost of living, neighborhoods, coworking, and the day-to-day reality of each base.

Every guide here is written from lived experience rather than scraped from a spreadsheet. Each one digs into what a place is actually like to base yourself in for a month or more: a realistic monthly budget, which neighbourhoods suit remote workers, where the wifi holds up, the coworking scene, and the small day-to-day frictions nobody warns you about. Read them alongside the hard numbers in our 410 city profiles and the best-cities rankings, put two places side by side, or thread several into one trip with the route planner.
