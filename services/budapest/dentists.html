Where these came from

11 of the 22 come from a French diplomatic mission. The others come from Dental Departures (6), hungary.diplomatie.belgium.be, bmeia.gv.at, a German diplomatic mission, the UK Foreign Office's travel advice and evergreendent.co.uk. 6 of these are from a commercial directory, which may sell placement and often makes one claim about a whole roster rather than about the named business. Read that tier with more caution than the rest. These lists are published without a guarantee of the service and naming a provider is not a recommendation by the government that published it. A directory listing is a weaker claim than a government one and may be paid placement.

15 of the 22 sit on an official list; the remaining 7 rest on 1 on the provider stating it and 6 on a directory listing.
