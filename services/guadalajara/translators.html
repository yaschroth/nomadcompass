Where these came from

118 of the 119 come from the Consejo de la Judicatura del Estado de Jalisco. The other one comes from bmeia.gv.at. Some of these are court-authorised translators and interpreters on the 2026-2027 list of the Consejo de la Judicatura del Estado de Jalisco, which authorises each for named language pairs and judicial districts. The languages shown are the pairs each is authorised in.
