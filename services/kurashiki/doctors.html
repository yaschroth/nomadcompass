This page holds every doctor in Kurashiki whose working language we can trace to a published source. If you know a provider that belongs here, tell us and include where the language is stated.

Where these came from

All 89 come from one place, Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. Some of these are on 医療情報ネット, the medical register Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare publishes from what each hospital and clinic is required by law to report about itself. Languages whose entry, in the facility's own words, relies on a translation device or interpreter, asks patients to call first or offers only a few words are left out, and in Tokyo, whose form also asks it, only languages the facility accepts patients in without prior consultation are listed.

All 89 sit on an official list, published by a body with nothing to sell.

If none of these fits

If none of these fits, the nearest doctors we list are in Okayama (195, 15 km away), Takamatsu (122, 38 km away) and Matsue (65, 117 km away). Kurashiki itself also has 61 dentists and 5 pharmacies listed. A gap here means we have not found a source we can cite, not that nobody exists.
