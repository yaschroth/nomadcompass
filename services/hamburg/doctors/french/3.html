Where these came from

All 674 come from one source, the Kassenärztliche Vereinigung Hamburg, Hamburg's statutory doctors' association, and every card links to it.

Every row here carries a French claim from its source: 674 of 674 from an official list, 0 self-declared and 0 from a directory. A language claim is not a claim about quality, and none of it is a recommendation.
