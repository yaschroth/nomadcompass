These come from 3 sources. the Kassenärztliche Vereinigung Hamburg, Hamburg's statutory doctors' association (3577), the UK Foreign Office's travel advice (2) and Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (1) between them, and every card links to the one it came from.

Every row here carries an English claim from its source: 3580 of 3580 from an official list, 0 self-declared and 0 from a directory. A language claim is not a claim about quality, and none of it is a recommendation.
