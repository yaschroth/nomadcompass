Where these came from

These come from 2 sources. the Colegio de Traductores Públicos de la Ciudad de Buenos Aires (2790) and the UK Foreign Office's Find a professional service abroad (5) between them, and every card links to the one it came from.

Every row here carries an English claim from its source: 2795 of 2795 from an official list, 0 self-declared and 0 from a directory. A language claim is not a claim about quality, and none of it is a recommendation.
