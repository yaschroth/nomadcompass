Where these came from

3322 of the 3327 come from the Colegio de Traductores Públicos de la Ciudad de Buenos Aires. The other 5 come from the UK Foreign Office's Find a professional service abroad. Some of these are listed on the UK Foreign Office's Find a professional service abroad, under translators and interpreters in Argentina. It is listed as providing official, sworn or certified work. Some of these are public translators on the roll of the Colegio de Traductores Públicos de la Ciudad de Buenos Aires, the body that licenses them under Ley 20.305. The languages shown are the ones each translator is licensed (matriculado) in.

All 3327 sit on an official list, the strongest tier this directory carries.

If none of these fits

If none of these fits, the nearest translators we list are in Montevideo (4, 205 km away), Rosario (593, 279 km away) and Mar del Plata (29, 386 km away).
