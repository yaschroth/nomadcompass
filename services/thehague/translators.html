Where these came from

All 101 come from one place, the Dutch register of sworn interpreters and translators (Rbtv, Bureau Wbtv). The Dutch Register of sworn interpreters and translators, kept by Bureau Wbtv under the Sworn Interpreters and Translators Act, lists each sworn translator and interpreter with the language pairs they are sworn in. The people below are sworn in the language shown, to or from Dutch.

All 101 sit on an official list, the strongest tier this directory carries.

If none of these fits

If none of these fits, the nearest translators we list are in Delft (8, 8 km away), Rotterdam (80, 20 km away) and Amsterdam (177, 53 km away). The Hague itself also has 11 lawyers and 1 doctor listed. A gap here means we have not found a source we can cite, not that nobody exists.
