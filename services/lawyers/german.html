Where these came from

These come from 39 different sources, led by a German diplomatic mission (97), a German diplomatic mission (76) and a German diplomatic mission (36). A consular list records the language because the mission publishes it for its own citizens, which is why German turns up in 36 countries rather than only where German is spoken.

538 of the 540 sit on an official list; the rest rest on the provider saying so or on a directory listing, which is a weaker claim and is labelled as such on each card.

Where we have nothing

We list lawyers in 177 further cities with no German speaker recorded, among them Vienna (92), Berlin (58), Istanbul (49) and Nairobi (45). That is a gap in what has been published, not proof that nobody there works in German.

Questions

How many German-speaking lawyers does this page list?
540, in 78 cities. That is the number whose working language we can trace to a published source.
Where are most of them?
Paris with 99, Budapest with 40 and Madrid with 39. Together that is 33% of the page.
Does a listing mean the lawyer speaks German personally?
Not always. Some sources name a practice whose staff include a German speaker rather than the named person, and each card quotes the wording its source used.
