This page lists every city where we can point at a source saying a lawyer works in French. If you know a provider that belongs here, tell us and include where the language is stated.

Where these came from

These come from 34 different sources, led by the French embassy in Spain (97), a German diplomatic mission (37) and at.usembassy.gov (23). A consular list records the language because the mission publishes it for its own citizens, which is why French turns up in 29 countries rather than only where French is spoken.

347 of the 350 sit on an official list; the rest rest on the provider saying so or on a directory listing, which is a weaker claim and is labelled as such on each card.

Where we have nothing

We list lawyers in 191 further cities with no French speaker recorded, among them Paris (210), Istanbul (49), Nairobi (45) and Munich (34). That is a gap in what has been published, not proof that nobody there works in French.

Questions

How many French-speaking lawyers does this page list?
350, in 64 cities. That is the number whose working language we can trace to a published source.
Where are most of them?
Barcelona with 54, Madrid with 52 and Vienna with 19. Together that is 36% of the page.
Does a listing mean the lawyer speaks French personally?
Not always. Some sources name a practice whose staff include a French speaker rather than the named person, and each card quotes the wording its source used.
