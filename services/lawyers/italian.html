Where these came from

These come from 26 different sources, led by an Italian consulate (267), an Italian consulate (47) and an Italian consulate (31). A consular list records the language because the mission publishes it for its own citizens, which is why Italian turns up in 17 countries rather than only where Italian is spoken.

453 of the 454 sit on an official list; the rest rest on the provider saying so or on a directory listing, which is a weaker claim and is labelled as such on each card.

Where we have nothing

We list lawyers in 215 further cities with no Italian speaker recorded, among them Rome (60), Florence (58), Milan (52) and Istanbul (49). That is a gap in what has been published, not proof that nobody there works in Italian.

Questions

How many Italian-speaking lawyers does this page list?
454, in 40 cities. That is the number whose working language we can trace to a published source.
Where are most of them?
Budapest with 267, Paris with 47 and Madrid with 28. Together that is 75% of the page.
Does a listing mean the lawyer speaks Italian personally?
Not always. Some sources name a practice whose staff include an Italian speaker rather than the named person, and each card quotes the wording its source used.
