Where these came from

44 of the 60 come from the Japan Federation of Bar Associations' lawyer search. The others come from the UK Foreign Office's Find a professional service abroad (7), an Italian consulate (5) and a Korean embassy or consulate (4). Some of these are listed on the UK FCDO's Find a professional service abroad, which lists lawyers abroad and says every one of them has confirmed it can provide services in English. Korean embassies and consulates publish these lists for Korean citizens abroad, naming local hospitals, lawyers and interpreters and, for each, whether they can serve in Korean (and sometimes English). The embassies say they do not vouch for the providers. Some of these lawyers are on ひまわりサーチ, the lawyer information service run by the Japan Federation of Bar Associations and each local bar association, where the lawyer states the foreign languages they work in. Languages the lawyer qualifies as conversational, basic or through an interpreter are left out.

All 60 sit on an official list, the strongest tier this directory carries.

Where in Osaka
