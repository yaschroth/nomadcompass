This page lists every city where we can point at a source saying a school teaches in German. If you know a provider that belongs here, tell us and include where the language is stated.

Where these came from

These come from 4 different sources, led by auslandsschulwesen.de (110), the French Ministry of Education (18) and juntadeandalucia.es (6). A consular list records the language because the mission publishes it for its own citizens, which is why German turns up in 59 countries rather than only where German is spoken.

Every one of these sits on an official list, published by a body with nothing to sell.

Where we have nothing

We list schools in 170 further cities with no German teaching recorded, among them Berlin (47), Beirut (23), Cádiz (22) and Calgary (21). That is a gap in what has been published, not proof that nobody there teaches in German.
