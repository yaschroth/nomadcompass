Where these came from

All 895 come from one place, the Chamber of Tax Advisers of the Czech Republic. Some of these are on the list of tax advisers kept by the Chamber of Tax Advisers of the Czech Republic (Komora daňových poradců ČR). Each adviser enters their own languages, and the Chamber publishes them without checking them.

All 895 sit on an official list, the strongest tier this directory carries.

Where in Prague

Spread across 297 postcodes: 2116 (34), 1734 (20), 2581 (20), 2027 (17), 2102 (10), and 292 more. 428 of the 895 give no postcode.

If none of these fits

If none of these fits, the nearest tax advisers we list are in Plzeň (50, 85 km away), Liberec (35, 89 km away) and Karlovy Vary (4, 113 km away). Prague itself also has 5991 lawyers, 408 translators, 14 doctors and 5 dentists and 1 more services listed. A gap here means we have not found a source we can cite, not that nobody exists.
