5616, on the sources we have read. That is what is published and checked, not what exists: a lawyer who speaks English and appears on no list is not here.
What if I need another language?
Prague also has 1677 in German, 572 in French and 572 in Russian, listed on the lawyers page for the city.
When was this checked?
The oldest entry here was checked in 20 August 2026 and the newest in 24 September 2026. 2173 of 5616 have a website of their own, which is the fastest way to confirm before you book.
