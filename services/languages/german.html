This page lists every city where a published source says a provider works in German. If you know a provider that belongs here, tell us and include where the language is stated.

Where these came from

These come from 146 different sources, led by the Slovak Bar Association (1018), the Polish Ministry of Justice (976) and the Romanian Ministry of Justice (867). Consular lists are the reason a language turns up far from where it is spoken: a mission publishes the doctors and lawyers its own citizens can talk to, which is a language claim someone else made in public and we can point at.

6963 of the 6987 sit on an official list. The rest rest on the provider saying so, or on a directory that says it of its whole roster, and is labelled as such on every card.

What this page is not

The coverage is not even across services: translators account for 3239 of the 6987, while opticians account for 1. That reflects which professions get published in language-tagged lists, not which ones exist.

Questions

How many German-speaking providers are listed?
6987, in 259 cities. That is the number whose working language we can trace to a published source, not an estimate of how many exist.
Where is German easiest to find?
Bratislava with 922, Bucharest with 877 and Prague with 402. Together that is 32% of this page.
Does a listing mean the provider speaks German personally?
Not always. Some sources name a practice whose staff include a German speaker rather than the named person, and each card quotes the wording its source used so you can judge it.
