This page lists every city where a published source says a provider works in Japanese. If you know a provider that belongs here, tell us and include where the language is stated.

Where these came from

These come from 60 different sources, led by the Romanian Ministry of Justice (104), Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (98) and the Victorian Legal Services Board + Commissioner (93). Consular lists are the reason a language turns up far from where it is spoken: a mission publishes the doctors and lawyers its own citizens can talk to, which is a language claim someone else made in public and we can point at.

786 of the 792 sit on an official list. The rest rest on the provider saying so, or on a directory that says it of its whole roster, and is labelled as such on every card.

What this page is not

The coverage is not even across services: translators account for 209 of the 792, while physiotherapists account for 2. That reflects which professions get published in language-tagged lists, not which ones exist.

Questions

How many Japanese-speaking providers are listed?
792, in 99 cities. That is the number whose working language we can trace to a published source, not an estimate of how many exist.
Where is Japanese easiest to find?
Seoul with 135, Bucharest with 105 and Melbourne with 93. Together that is 42% of this page.
Does a listing mean the provider speaks Japanese personally?
Not always. Some sources name a practice whose staff include a Japanese speaker rather than the named person, and each card quotes the wording its source used so you can judge it.
