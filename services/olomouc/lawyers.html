Where these came from

170 of the 179 come from the Czech Bar Association. The others come from the Notarial Chamber of the Czech Republic (7) and the UK Foreign Office's Find a professional service abroad (2). Some of these are notaries on the list kept by the Notarial Chamber of the Czech Republic, which records the foreign languages each notary works in. Some of these are on the register of lawyers kept by the Czech Bar Association (Česká advokátní komora). The languages are the ones each lawyer reported to the Bar, which publishes them without checking them.

All 179 sit on an official list, the strongest tier this directory carries.
