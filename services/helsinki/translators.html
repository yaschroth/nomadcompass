Where these came from

All 373 come from one place, the Finnish National Agency for Education (authorised translators). Some of these are on the register of authorised translators kept by Finland's National Agency for Education. Each language is one the translator is authorised to translate to or from Finnish or Swedish.

All 373 sit on an official list, the strongest tier this directory carries.

If none of these fits

If none of these fits, the nearest translators we list are in Tallinn (63, 82 km away), Turku (68, 150 km away) and Tampere (56, 161 km away). Helsinki itself also has 4 doctors, 2 dentists and 2 therapists listed. A gap here means we have not found a source we can cite, not that nobody exists.
